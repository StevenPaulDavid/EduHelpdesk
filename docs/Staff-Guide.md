# Reporting a problem — a guide for staff

The guide for staff now lives inside EduHelpdesk, so it always matches the version your school has installed.

1. Open your school's helpdesk address in a browser. It ends in `/Portal` - ask the IT team if you're not sure what it is.
2. Press **? Help** in the top bar.

It covers signing in, reporting a problem, what counts as urgent, following up a ticket, replying and adding photos, notifications, requesting a purchasing project, and changing your password.

Your school can add its own note to the top of that page - who to ring for something urgent, for example. (IT staff: add it from the helpdesk's Help centre, on the article *The staff portal*.)
