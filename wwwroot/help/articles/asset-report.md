A snapshot of the asset register. At the top, choose the **warranty window** (how far ahead to look for warranties ending), the period **problem devices** are counted over, and the **desk**.

## The sections

- **Assets to review** - warranty ending or replacement due within the review window (or already past), overdue loans, leavers' kit, and DfE checks or end of support coming up, each with why it needs review.
- **Assets by type, make, location and status** - counts and shares.
- **How old the fleet is** - by type: how many have a purchase date, average age, the oldest, and how many are due or overdue for replacement. Assets with no purchase date are left out of the ages, and the report says how many.
- **Replacement planning** - assets due or overdue for replacement, and those due in the next 12 months: a starting point for next year's budget. Downloadable as CSV.
- **Warranty ending** - within the window, and those already out of warranty.
- **Assets with the most tickets** - devices that keep coming back are candidates for replacement.

The review windows are set in **Settings → Asset, part & loan rules**.
