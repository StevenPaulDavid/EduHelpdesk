If your role is about oversight rather than fixing things, you'll mostly use **Reports** and, for data protection, the **audit log**. Your menu only shows the reports your role is allowed to open.

## Reports

Open **Reports** from the top menu. The tabs across the top are the reports your role can read, for example:

| Report | What it answers |
| --- | --- |
| Asset reports | What the school owns, how old it is, warranties ending, devices due for review |
| Ticket reports | SLA performance, workload, repeat problems |
| Finance | Spend, orders and disposals by academic year, for the auditors |
| Job spend | What site jobs cost, by budget code, desk, month and supplier |
| Premises checks | Every statutory check done, on time or not, with readings and failures |
| Cleaning rounds | Rooms missed and spot-check scores |

With the *Export and print reports* tick, each report can be downloaded as a spreadsheet (CSV) and printed or saved as PDF with the school's logo.

## The audit log

**Settings → Audit log** (or **Audit log** in the top menu, if that's all you have) lists every change: who, when, and what it was before and after. Filter it by area, action, who made the change, or dates.

## Personal data

- For a subject access request, open the person from **People** and use **Download their data (.zip)** under *Subject access*: their record, the tickets they raised with every update, attached files and assets.
- Leavers' personal data is removed on the schedule set in **Settings → Data retention**.
- Every look at the raw database is itself recorded in the audit log.
