The **board** shows the same tickets as the list, as cards in columns. It's the quick way to see where everything stands, and to share out or move on a lot of jobs.

## Opening it

On the **Tickets** page, press **Board** in the **List | Board** switch beside the queues. **List** takes you back. The queue you're on, the search and the filters stay as they are, so the board shows exactly the tickets the list did.

## Columns

Choose the columns with **Status | Technician** above the board:

- **Status** - a column for each status your desk uses, and **Closed** at the end. Closed shows tickets closed in the last 7 days.
- **Technician** - **Unassigned**, then a column for each person on the desks being shown. Handy for sharing out the morning's jobs.

The number beside each heading is how many tickets are in the column. A column draws up to 60 cards; when there are more, **Open as a list** shows them all.

## What a card shows

The ticket's number and title (click to open it), who reported it and the room, its priority, and badges for a requester's **Reply**, when it's due, and the day it's **planned** for. A **red bar** down the left means overdue; **amber** means due soon.

## Moving a card

You need permission to edit tickets.

- **Drag** the card to another column and let go.
- Or press **Move** on the card, choose the column and press **Move**. This works with a keyboard, and on a phone or tablet where dragging doesn't.

On the Status board, moving a card changes its status. On the Technician board it gives the ticket to that person, or to nobody if dropped on **Unassigned**. The change is written to the ticket's History, as if you'd made it on the ticket.

The same rules apply as on the ticket page. If a move isn't allowed, the card stays put and a message says why - for example, the person isn't in the ticket's team, or the status belongs to another desk.

## Closing from the board

Drop a card on **Closed** to close it.

- If the ticket's priority or category needs a **closing message**, a box asks for it. The requester sees the message in the staff portal. See [Closing a ticket](close-a-ticket).
- A **premises check** can't be closed until its checklist is filled in; open the ticket to do that.

Drag a card out of **Closed** to reopen it in that status.

> **Tip:** Filter first, then switch to the board. *My tickets* by Status is your own to-do board; *Unassigned* by Technician is the jobs waiting to be shared out.
