## Name, wording and colours

- **Brand name** - shown in the top bar and on print-outs, such as *Oakfield Helpdesk*.
- **Overview eyebrow, title and description** - the wording at the top of the Overview page.
- **Primary colour** - buttons, links and headings.
- **Accent colour** - behind secondary buttons and highlights.
- **Background colour** - behind every page.

Colours are six-digit hex values, such as `#067A78`. In dark mode the background and accent are swapped for dark ones and the primary is lightened for text, so any colour stays readable.

## Default appearance

**Light**, **Dark** or **Match device**, for devices that haven't chosen. Anyone can pick their own from the **Appearance** switch at the bottom of every page, including the staff portal, and their choice wins on that device. *Match device* follows the computer's own light or dark setting.

## School logo

Upload a **PNG** to show in the top bar on every page (sign-in and the staff portal included), on printed tickets and on report print-outs, in place of the initials badge.

- A transparent background works best.
- It's shown about 40 pixels tall, so a simple crest or badge reads better than a wide banner.
- In dark mode it sits on a small light panel, so dark lettering still shows.

**Replace logo** uploads a new one; **Remove logo** goes back to the initials.
