**Facilities** is the built-in desk for the site team: repairs, premises checks and cleaning (see [Desks](desks)). While you're working on it, the top bar has a **Facilities** menu. Each item shows only if your role can open it.

| Item | What it's for | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Premises checks** | The checks to do now, what's coming up and how each went last time | Tickets, and the Premises checks report |
| **Cleaning rounds** | Printing the day's sheets and ticking off the rooms | Cleaning rounds |
| **Job spend** | What jobs cost, by budget code, month and supplier | The Job spend report |
| **Contractors** | The contractors the site team calls out, by trade | Suppliers |

## Premises checks

A premises check - the weekly fire alarm test, the monthly water temperatures - is a recurring ticket with a checklist, set up in **Settings → Recurring tickets & checks** (open to **Set up facilities** as well as settings). Each time it's due it logs a ticket with its own copy of the checklist.

**Facilities → Premises checks** shows:

- **To do** - open checks, overdue first, then due today, then due later, with how many lines are filled in. Open one to fill in its checklist and close it.
- **Coming up** - when each check next logs a ticket, this week and in the next month.
- **Last time** - how each check went when it was last closed: all passed or how many failed, and whether it was late.

**Yearly log** opens the premises compliance log (Reports → Premises checks), for governors, insurers and inspections.

## Contractors

Any supplier can be marked as a **contractor**, with its **trade** (Electrician, Plumber, Fire safety…), on its edit page under **Assets → Suppliers**. **Facilities → Contractors** lists them by trade - the same supplier directory, so a company that both sells kit and fixes the boiler is only in it once.
