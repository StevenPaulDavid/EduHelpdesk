**People** holds three directories, each on its own tab. You see the tabs your role can open.

| Tab | Who's in it |
| --- | --- |
| Users | **Requesters** - staff who report problems in the staff portal |
| Technicians | **Staff accounts** - helpdesk logins. See [Staff accounts](staff-accounts) |
| Roles | What each kind of staff account can do. See [Roles and permissions](roles-and-permissions) |

Filter a tab by department or team and by status (active or inactive), and search by name or email.

## A requester's details

Open a requester to see and change:

- **Name**, **Email**, **Department**, **Location** (room), **Type** and **Start date**.
- **Active account?** - untick when they leave. They can no longer use the staff portal; their tickets stay. **Left on** records the date.
- **Can raise projects** - for line managers and SLT, to ask IT to source and quote for purchases from the portal.
- **Project lead** - confirms each project's priority and assigns the technician, from the portal.
- **Set a password** / **Give them a temporary password** - their staff portal password.
- **Select tickets** - tick tickets to make this person their requester.

## When someone leaves

The requester's page shows everything still **held by** them: assets, loan kits, open tickets, active projects and live access on the access control register. **Book everything back in** and **Remove all access** sort the assets and access in one go. Pass open tickets to whoever took over.

After the period set in **Settings → Data retention**, a leaver's personal details are anonymised.

## Subject access

**Download their data (.zip)** gathers everything the helpdesk holds about a person, for a subject access request: their record, the tickets they raised with every update and internal note, files, assets and loans, projects, other tickets that mention them, and the audit lines about them.
