The cyber security standard asks schools to review who has access **every term**. Do it with the business manager or finance team: keep what's still needed and remove what isn't. The recorded review is the evidence.

## Doing a review

1. Open **Reports → Access review** (or the **Termly review** tab on the register).
2. Choose to review **every system and area**, or just one, and press **Show**.
3. Go down the list. **Everything is kept unless you tick Remove.** Anything flagged in red - a leaver, privileged access nobody approved, MFA off - is worth removing or fixing now.
4. Under **Record the review**, fill in the date, who you **reviewed with**, a **reason** for anything removed, and any notes.
5. Press **Record the review**. Ticked access is removed, the rest is marked as reviewed today.

**Past reviews** lists each one: when, with whom, by whom, what it covered, and how many were kept and removed.

## How often

**Review every (days)** sets how often a review is due - about **120 days** is once a term. Access not reviewed within that shows as *review overdue* on the register and the compliance summary, and sends a reminder under the bell.
