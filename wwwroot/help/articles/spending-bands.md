**Spending bands** are your finance policy's thresholds: how many quotes a purchase needs, and what else it has to meet, at each level of spend. Every project shows them for reference, with its current band highlighted.

## The bands

Add one per threshold:

- **Name** - such as *Band B*.
- **From (£)** and **Up to (£)** - both ends count, to the penny, so one band ends at 999.99 and the next starts at 1,000. Leave *Up to* blank for the top band. Bands can't overlap; gaps are allowed.
- **Quotes needed** - such as 3.
- **Requirements** - anything else, such as *Business manager sign-off* or *Tender via the Trust*.

## Which total a project is banded on

A project is placed in a band by the **highest quote for each of its items**, added up over the **whole contract** (every payment over the full term). So the band shows as soon as prices come in, before any quote is chosen, and choosing a cheaper quote doesn't drop the project into a band that needs fewer quotes. Choose whether that total is **excluding** or **including VAT**.

## Amounts on a project's page

Whether a project's page shows quote figures **excluding VAT** (what most schools budget on) or **including VAT** with the ex-VAT figures alongside. The proposal PDF always shows both, and the projects report is unaffected.

Purchasing requirements (the tick boxes a requester picks, such as *Lease / finance option*) are a simple list in **Settings → Purchasing requirements**.
