The **daily sheet** lists the open jobs room by room, with a box to tick and space to write, so someone without a computer can work through them and hand the sheet back.

## Printing it

1. Open **Tickets**.
2. If you want only some jobs, use **Filters** first: tick the desk (such as *Site*), or choose a team.
3. Press **Daily sheet**. It opens in a new tab with the jobs for the desks and team shown, ready to print or save as PDF.

Overdue jobs are shaded so they stand out. Each job shows its number, what it is, its priority, when it's due and who it's assigned to.

## When the sheet comes back

Update each job on its ticket: add a reply with what was done, and **Mark as closed** the finished ones. Search for a job's number on the Tickets page to find it quickly.

> **Tip:** For cleaning, use the **cleaning round** sheets instead - they list every room on the round, not just the ones with a problem. See [Getting started: cleaning rounds](getting-started-cleaning).
