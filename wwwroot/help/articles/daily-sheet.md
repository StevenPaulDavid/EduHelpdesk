The **daily sheet** lists the open jobs room by room, with a box to tick and space to write, so someone without a computer can work through them and hand the sheet back.

## Printing it

1. Open **Tickets**.
2. If you want only some jobs, use **Filters** first: tick the desk (such as *Site*), or choose a team.
3. Choose **More → Print the daily sheet**. It opens in a new tab with the jobs for the desks and team shown, ready to print or save as PDF.

Overdue jobs are shaded so they stand out. Each job shows its number, what it is, its priority, when it's due and who it's assigned to.

## A day's planned jobs

The sheet can also be the plan for one day: just the jobs **planned for** it (see [Planning a ticket for a day](plan-a-ticket)).

- **Today's** - **Print today's sheets** on the overview, or **More → Print today's planned jobs** on the Tickets page.
- **Another day** - **Print** under the day's name on the planner's week. See [The ticket planner](ticket-planner).

These open with **a page for each person**, so each technician or caretaker takes their own, with the jobs nobody has yet on the last page.

**Today's** sheet also carries any job planned for an earlier day that still isn't done, marked **From** that day, so nothing that slipped is left off the next morning's round.

At the top of the sheet (it isn't printed) you can change the **day**, choose one **person** or *Unassigned*, and turn **A page for each person** on or off. Leave the day empty for every open job, as before.

## When the sheet comes back

Update each job on its ticket: add a reply with what was done, and **Mark as closed** the finished ones. Search for a job's number on the Tickets page to find it quickly.

> **Tip:** For cleaning, use the **cleaning round** sheets instead - they list every room on the round, not just the ones with a problem. See [Getting started: cleaning rounds](getting-started-cleaning).
