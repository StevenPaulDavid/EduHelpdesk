A **cleaning round** is a set of rooms cleaned on the same days, usually by the same cleaner. Cleaners don't need to sign in: they work from a printed sheet, and the supervisor ticks off on screen what was done when the sheets come back.

## Setting up a round

Rounds are set up in **Settings → Cleaning rounds** (the gear in the top bar) by someone who can change settings or whose role has **Set up facilities**. First make sure:

- every room is in **Settings → Departments, buildings & rooms → Locations**, and
- every cleaner's name is in **Settings → Cleaners**.

Then add a round:

1. Give it a name, such as *Ground floor classrooms*.
2. Choose the **usual cleaner**. Their name is filled in against each room when you tick the round off, and you can change it on the day if someone else covered.
3. Set **Counted from**: the first day the round is due. Days before it never show as missed.
4. Tick the **days** it's done and the **rooms** it covers. Rooms already on another round show that round's name, so it's easy to spot one left out.

To stop a round for a while (over the summer, say) without losing what was recorded, untick **In use**.

## Printing the day's sheets

Open **Facilities → Cleaning rounds** in the top bar. It shows the rounds due today. Press **Print sheet** on each and hand it to the cleaner: it lists the rooms with a box to tick and space for a note.

## Ticking off a round

When the sheets come back, open **Cleaning rounds** again (use the day picker or the arrows for an earlier day). For each room:

- tick **Done** if it was cleaned, and check the **cleaner**,
- add a **note** for any room that wasn't done, such as *Room locked*,
- optionally give a **spot check** score from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent) when you've inspected the room, with a note.

**Tick all done** ticks every room at once for the usual day when the whole sheet comes back done. Then press **Save**.

> **Important:** A room left unticked on a day you saved counts as **missed**. A day where nothing was saved at all isn't counted as missed - it's listed separately, because it's usually a holiday.

## The rounds report

**Reports → Cleaning rounds** shows, for the days you choose:

- rooms done and missed, by round and by room, with the notes left,
- spot-check averages, lowest first, so problem rooms stand out,
- the days nothing was recorded.

It can be printed or saved as PDF for the business manager or the cleaning contractor, and downloaded as a spreadsheet (CSV).
