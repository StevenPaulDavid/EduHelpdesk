The DfE digital and technology standards ask for an asset register that records **when each asset was last checked**, **when its next check is due**, and **when it stops being supported**. EduHelpdesk keeps all three and flags what's coming up.

## Recording a check

- **One asset** - open it, choose **Checks & support**, fill in **Checked on** and **By**, and press **Record a check**.
- **Many at once** - in the register, tick the assets, choose **Record a check** and apply. Handy after a walk round a classroom.

Recording a check sets the **next check** a number of months later, set in **Settings → Asset, part & loan rules**.

## End of support

**End of support** is when the asset stops getting security updates (an old version of Windows, a switch the maker has dropped), or, for a leased or loaned asset, when the lease ends. It's separate from the **replacement date**, which is for budgeting.

## What gets flagged

An asset comes up **for review** - on the Overview, under *Needs review* in the register, and in the compliance summary - when:

- its warranty ends, or it's due for replacement, within the review window,
- its check is overdue or due within the *check due soon* window,
- its support or lease ends within the *support ending* window.

All three windows are set in **Settings → Asset, part & loan rules**. Overdue checks and ending support also send a reminder under the bell to people who can open assets. See [The compliance summary](compliance-summary).
