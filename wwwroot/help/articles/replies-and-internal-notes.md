The reply box is at the top of a ticket's **Comments** tab.

## Reply or internal note

Choose one before you write:

- **Reply** - goes to the requester. They see it when they open the ticket in the staff portal, and it's printed on the ticket.
- **Internal note** - for the helpdesk only. The requester never sees it, and it's left off printed tickets and the requester's PDF copy. Use it for passwords you tried, supplier references, or anything not for the requester.

Internal notes show with an **Internal note** badge in the conversation.

## Changing the status as you reply

The **Status** box beside the button saves a new status with your reply, for example *On Hold* while you wait for a part. Some statuses stop the SLA clock while the ticket waits. See [Due dates and SLAs](due-dates-and-slas#statuses-that-pause-the-clock).

## Notifying the requester

Tick **Notify the requester** to send them a pop-up in the staff portal about your reply, if they've switched pop-ups on there. Leave it unticked for a small update that doesn't need their attention. Internal notes never notify anyone.

## Sharing files

On the **Attachments** tab, **Attach files** uploads photos or documents. Tick **Share with the requester** to let them see the file in the staff portal; otherwise it's kept for the helpdesk only. You can change this later from the attachment list.

## Removing a comment

A comment can be removed if it was posted by mistake. It can't be put back, and the history keeps a line saying a comment was removed and by whom.
