**Assets → Loans** shows everything out with staff right now - loan kits and individual devices - and what's come back lately.

## Issuing a loan

Press **Issue a loan**:

1. **What are you lending?** - a **loan kit** or a **single device**. Devices already held by someone, and anything in a kit, aren't offered.
2. **Who is borrowing it?** - someone from the staff directory. For a kit only, choose *Not listed* and type a name for supply staff or visitors; a single device must go to someone in the directory so it can be traced.
3. **Why do they need it?** - the reason, from the list in **Settings → Lists → Loan reasons** (*Laptop in for repair*, *Forgot their charger*...).
4. **Due back** - starts at today; push it out if they need it longer.
5. Any **notes**, then **Issue**.

You can also lend a single device from its own page (**Ownership & loans → Loan out**).

## Booking it back in

Press **Book back in** beside the loan. To choose the **status** a device comes back with (*In repair* if it came back broken, say), return it from the device's own page instead: **Ownership & loans → Return device**.

## Overdue loans

The figures at the top show how many are out and how many are past their due-back date. Tick **Overdue only** to list just those. Overdue loans are also listed on the Overview.

## The loan report

**Loan report** shows who borrows most often. It flags anyone who has taken a set number of kits within a set number of days (**Settings → Asset, part & loan rules**), so a line manager can be given a short list worth following up.
