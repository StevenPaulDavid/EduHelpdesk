EduHelpdesk is where staff report problems and where you keep track of the jobs: what needs doing, who's doing it, and what happened. This guide covers what you'll use every day.

![The overview page after signing in](overview.png)

## The menu along the top

- **Overview** - your dashboard: open tickets, what's overdue, and what's waiting for you.
- **Tickets** - every job you can work on. Most of your day is here.
- **Log a ticket** (the green button) - record a job yourself, for example when someone stops you in the corridor.
- **The bell** - new tickets and replies from staff. A number shows how many you haven't looked at.
- **?** - this help.
- **Your name** - change your password, set up two-step sign-in, choose your notifications, and sign out.

You only see the parts of EduHelpdesk your role allows, so a colleague's menu may look different from yours.

## Finding your tickets

Open **Tickets**. The tabs along the top are the queues:

| Tab | What's in it |
| --- | --- |
| Open | Every ticket that isn't closed |
| My tickets | Tickets assigned to you |
| Unassigned | Tickets nobody has picked up yet |
| Overdue / due soon | Tickets past their due date, or close to it |
| All tickets | Closed ones too |

![The ticket list](tickets-list.png)

Search by number, title, requester or asset tag, or use **Filters** to narrow the list by status, priority, category, team, location and more. See [The ticket list](ticket-list).

> **Tip:** If the school has more than one desk (IT, Site, Cleaning), the list shows your own desks' tickets. Other desks' tickets aren't shown to you unless your role has "See all desks".

## Working a ticket

Click a ticket's number or title to open it. From the bar at the top you can:

1. **Assign to me** - take the job, so others know it's yours.
2. **On my way** - takes the job and tells the person who reported it that you're coming.
3. Change the **status**, priority or category with the pencil beside each in the side panel.
4. **Add reply** - choose **Reply** to write to the person who reported it, or **Internal note** for something only the helpdesk sees. Tick **Notify the requester** if they should get a pop-up about your reply.
5. **Mark as closed** when it's done. Some tickets ask for a closing message, which the requester sees. See [Closing a ticket](close-a-ticket).

The tabs beside **Comments** hold the ticket's **History** (every change, by whom), linked **Assets**, **Parts** used, **Costs**, and **Attachments**.

## Logging a job yourself

Use **Log a ticket** for something that still needs doing. For a job you've already dealt with (a spill you mopped up, a door you fixed on the spot), use **Log a job already done** on the Tickets page instead. It's saved as a closed ticket with no deadline, so it still counts in the reports.

## Getting help

Every page with a guide has a **Help** link next to its title, and a **?** beside a field explains what to put in it. Search all the help from the **?** in the top bar.
