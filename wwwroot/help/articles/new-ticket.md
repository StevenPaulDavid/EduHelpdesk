Use **Log a ticket** in the top bar for a job that still needs doing. (Staff usually report their own problems through the staff portal; this form is for jobs you log yourself.)

With more than one desk, **Log a ticket** goes to the desk you're working on: **Log a Site ticket** starts the form on the Site desk's categories. On **My desks** or **All desks** it's a menu, so you pick the desk first. See [Desks](desks).

![The new ticket form](new-ticket.png)

## Start from a template

If your school has ticket templates, **Start from a template** fills in the form for a common job, such as a new starter's laptop. Everything can still be changed afterwards. Templates are set up in **Settings → Ticket templates**.

## The issue

- **Requester** - the person the job is for. Start typing a name, email or department to find them.
- **Ticket title** - a short summary, such as *Projector has no signal in B12*.
- **Description** - what's wrong, what's been tried, and what needs doing next.

## Classification

- **Type** - an *incident* is something broken; a *request* is something being asked for.
- **Priority** - how urgent it is. With an SLA, the priority usually decides how long the ticket has.
- **Desk** - only shown when there's more than one. It narrows the categories, teams and people to that desk's.
- **Category** - what the ticket is about. The category also decides the desk and its ticket number (IT-12, SITE-4).
- **Location** - the room.
- **Assets** - the devices involved. Link as many as you need, or none.

## Assignment & scheduling

- **Assigned team** and **Assigned technician** - who's doing it. Choosing a team narrows the technicians to that team's people. Leave both blank and the ticket goes into **Unassigned**.
- **SLA** - how long the ticket has. See [Due dates and SLAs](due-dates-and-slas).
- **Due date override** - leave blank to work the due date out from the SLA. Fill it in only to set a date by hand.

## Additional details

Extra fields your school has added for some categories appear here once you choose one of those categories.

Press **Create ticket**. The requester can follow it in the staff portal.
