A ticket's **Planned for** date is the day the team means to do the job: the projector in M4 on Thursday, when the room is free. It's the helpdesk's own plan.

- **The requester never sees it.** It isn't shown in the staff portal or on the requester's copy of a ticket.
- **It doesn't change the due date or the SLA.** The due date is when the job should be done by; the planned day is when you mean to do it.

## Planning one ticket

1. Open the ticket.
2. Under **Assignment & scheduling**, click the pencil beside **Planned for**.
3. Choose the day and press the tick.

To take a ticket off the plan, clear the date and press the tick. Every change is kept in the ticket's **History**.

## Planning several at once

On the Tickets page, tick the tickets, choose **Plan for a day** under **With selected tickets**, pick the day and press **Apply to selected**. Leave the date empty to take them off the plan. Closed tickets are skipped. See [Updating many tickets at once](update-many-tickets).

## Planning by dragging

The **planner** shows the week by person, or the month, and lets you drag tickets onto days. See [The ticket planner](ticket-planner).

## Finding what's planned

The ticket list has a **Planned** column: *Today*, *Tomorrow*, or the day and date. Click its heading to sort by it.

In **Filters**, **Planned for** narrows the list to:

| Choice | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| Today | Tickets planned for today |
| This week | Tickets planned for Monday to Sunday of this week |
| Planned day gone by | Open tickets planned for a day that has passed |
| Not planned | Open tickets with no planned day |

## When the plan slips

- **Not done** - the planned day has gone by and the ticket is still open. Plan it for another day, or close it.
- **After it's due** - on the ticket page, the planned day is later than the due date. Bring it forward, or change the due date if the later day is agreed.

A closed ticket keeps its planned day as a record, but can't be given a new one until it's reopened.

> **Tip:** The export to CSV has a **Planned for** column, and the **Full report** PDF shows the planned day. The **Requester copy** leaves it out.
