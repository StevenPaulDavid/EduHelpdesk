Site jobs often cost money: a plumber's call-out, a replacement lock. The **Costs** tab on a ticket records it, so the business manager can see spend by budget code. You need the *See and add job costs* tick on your role to see the tab, and permission to edit tickets to add or remove lines.

## Adding a cost

Open the ticket, choose the **Costs** tab, fill in the boxes and press **Add cost**:

| Box | What to put |
| --- | --- |
| What it was for | Such as *Plumber call-out* |
| Date | When it was spent |
| Net (before VAT), £ | The amount before VAT, as on the invoice |
| VAT, £ | The VAT on it, as on the invoice, or blank if there's none |
| Supplier | From the suppliers list, if it's one of yours |
| Budget code | What it's charged to, such as *Reactive maintenance* |

A job can have as many cost lines as it needs; each shows its net, VAT and gross. Every line added or removed is recorded in the ticket's history.

## Budget codes

The list of budget codes is set in **Settings → Budget codes**. A code that's been used can't be deleted, but it can be renamed.

## The Job spend report

**Reports → Job spend** totals the costs by budget code, desk, month and supplier, with net, VAT and gross. It needs its own tick on the role.
