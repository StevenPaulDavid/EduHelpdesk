**Inventory → Assets** is the school's register of equipment: laptops, projectors, switches, and - with the Site desk - plant such as boilers and extinguishers. Every asset keeps its tickets, loans, checks and history together.

![The asset register](asset-register.png)

## Finding assets

Search by tag, serial number, make, model, building, room, holder or purchase order, and use **Filters** to narrow by status, type, make, building, room, who holds it, or desk.

**Show** picks out assets that need something:

| Show | Assets |
| --- | --- |
| Needs review | Warranty ending or replacement due soon, or already past |
| On loan / Loan overdue | Out with someone, or past their due-back date |
| Check overdue or due soon | Their DfE check is due |
| Unsupported or support ending | No more security updates soon, or a lease ending |
| Disposed | Sold, recycled or written off - kept for the record |

With more than one desk, the list starts on **your desks' asset types** (IT's laptops, Site's boilers). Choose *Every desk* in the Desk filter to see the rest. Assets aren't hidden from anyone; it just keeps the list relevant.

## Columns

**Columns** chooses which columns the list shows - purchase details, warranty, next check, end of support, condition and so on. Your choice is remembered on your account. Click a heading to sort by it.

## The buttons at the top

- **Add asset** - one at a time. See [The asset page](asset-page).
- **Import from CSV** - many at once, from a spreadsheet or the DfE register template. See [Importing assets](import-assets).

## Changing many at once

Tick the assets, choose an **Action** and press **Apply to selected**:

- **Change status** - such as *In repair* or *Spare*.
- **Reassign owner** - give them to a person, or to nobody.
- **Change room**.
- **Record a check** - the DfE check, with the date and who did it, for every selected asset. See [Asset checks and end of support](asset-checks).
- **Export to CSV** - download the selected assets.
