All of this is on a project's **Items & quotes** tab.

## Items

An **item** is one of the main things being bought, with a quantity - *30 × iPad*, *1 × charging trolley*. **Create items from the request** turns the request into items; **Add an item** adds one.

Each item can list what it **comes with** - keyboards, cases, pencils, screen protectors. These **sub-items** are an unpriced checklist; the item's price covers them. Use the arrows to put items in order.

## Suppliers

**Add supplier** puts a supplier on an item, from the directory or as a new one (name and optional email, added to the directory). Tick **Add to every item on this project** to ask the same supplier about everything.

Each supplier on an item has a **status**, date-stamped every time it changes:

*Not requested → Requested → Received*, then *Update requested → Update received* if you ask for a revised quote, or *Declined / no response*.

A quote **awaited for 7 days** or more is flagged to chase.

## Adding their quote

Open the supplier's row (**Add quote**):

- **Valid until** - when the quote expires; expired quotes are flagged.
- **Reference** - the supplier's quote number.
- **Files** - upload the quote (PDF, picture or document). Uploading marks it **Received**.
- **Prices** - one line per kind of payment, each with **what for**, the **amount** per payment **excluding VAT**, **how often** (one-off, monthly, quarterly, annually), for how many **years**, and its **VAT** (standard 20%, reduced 5%, zero-rated or no VAT). A lease might be a one-off set-up fee plus a monthly payment for 3 years.

The row shows each quote's **first year** and **whole term** totals, ex and inc VAT.

## When a supplier updates their quote

Set the status to *Update requested*. The next upload **replaces** the files and clears the prices for the new ones. Tick **Keep the current files and prices under Previous versions** to keep the old quote for reference.

## Choosing a quote

Press **Choose this quote** on the one to go with, or **Choose the cheapest** to pick the lowest. The chosen quotes make the project's totals and are marked in the proposal. **Unchoose** to change your mind.
