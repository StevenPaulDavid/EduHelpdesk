**Projects** in the top bar lists purchasing projects. Click one to open it.

## Views

| Tab | Projects |
| --- | --- |
| Active | Everything not closed |
| Awaiting assignment | Open projects the project lead hasn't given to anyone yet |
| Mine | Assigned to you |
| Due soon or late | Still waiting on a proposal, past their needed-by date or due within a few days |
| Closed | Decided |
| All | Everything |

## Filters

Narrow the list by **technician**, **priority** and **status**, and choose how to **sort** it and how many rows a page shows.

## The columns

Each row shows who **requested** it, the **technician**, the **priority** (marked *suggested* until the lead confirms it), the **status**, how many **quotes** are in, the **needed-by** date, and when it was last updated. Late or nearly due projects are highlighted.

See [How purchasing projects work](how-projects-work) for the whole process.
