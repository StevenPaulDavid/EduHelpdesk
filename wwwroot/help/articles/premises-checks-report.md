The compliance log: every check with a checklist logged in the **academic year** - fire alarm tests, water temperatures, emergency lighting - grouped by recurring ticket.

For each check it shows the ticket, when it was **logged**, when it was **due** and **done**, who did it, and the **result**: *All passed*, how many **failed**, or still *in progress*. Open the result to see every line and reading. Late checks are marked.

The heading of each group says how many were done, how many on time, and how many had failures.

**Print / Save as PDF** gives a log for governors, insurers or an inspection; **CSV of every line** downloads every reading.

See [Doing a premises check](premises-checks).
