**Systems and areas** (a tab on the access control register) is everything access can be given to. Add one with **Add a system or area**:

- **Name** - such as *Arbor*, *Microsoft 365*, *Server room*.
- **Kind** - a **system**, or a **physical area or key**.
- **Category** - from **Settings → Contract & access lists → System and area categories**.
- **Owner / approver** - who decides who gets access.
- **Cloud or remote access - MFA required for everyone** - tick for anything reached from the cloud or from home. Anyone recorded with access without MFA is then flagged.
- **Holds personal data** - for the data protection side.
- **Contract** - its entry on the contracts register, if it has one.
- **Notes**.

**Retired** stops new access being recorded while keeping its history. A system can only be deleted if nobody has ever had access to it.
