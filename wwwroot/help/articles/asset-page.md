Open an asset from the register to see everything about it, on tabs.

**New ticket**, beside the asset's name, logs a job for it: the form opens with the asset linked and you as the requester - change the requester if someone else asked for it. It's there for anyone who can log tickets, and not once the asset has been disposed of.

**Dispose via ticket**, beside it, does the same with the ticket ticked to dispose of the asset when it's closed. See [Disposing of assets through a ticket](dispose-via-a-ticket). While it waits, the asset page says which ticket it's on.

## Details

- **Asset tag** - the school's label number. Each tag is unique.
- **Status** - such as *In use*, *Spare*, *In repair*.
- **Make**, **model** and **type** - a model linked to a make is only offered for that make. The type decides which desk the asset belongs to.
- **Serial number** and **operating system**.
- **Building** and **room** - where it lives.
- **Condition** - new, used, refurbished, donated (for the DfE register).
- **Ownership** - owned by the school, or **leased** / **loaned** from someone else.
- **Assigned user** - who it's issued to. An assigned asset can't be lent out or put in a kit until it's returned.
- Any **custom fields** your school added for its type.

## Purchase & warranty

**Supplier**, **purchase date**, **price**, **purchase order** (the Trust's PO, if you were given one), **quote reference** (often the only reference for an order placed via the Trust), **warranty end** and **replacement date**. Leave the replacement date blank to work it out from the type's expected lifespan. A leased asset can be linked to its **contract** on the contracts register.

## Checks & support

The DfE check (last checked, by whom, next due) and **end of support** - when it stops getting security updates, or a lease ends. A ⚠ on the tab means one is due. See [Asset checks and end of support](asset-checks).

## Ownership & loans

Who holds it now, **Loan out** to lend it to someone with a reason and due-back date, **Return device** to book it back in, and the loan history. **Record a disposal** is here too. See [Disposing of an asset](dispose-an-asset).

### Devices with students

**Give to a student** is for a device a pupil keeps, such as a 1:1 laptop. Type the student's name: pupils aren't in People, so only the name is kept. The status becomes **With Student**, and the student appears under **Who has held it** like anyone else. It isn't a loan, so there's no due-back date and it never shows in Loans or the loan report.

**Back from student** puts it back to stock. Changing the status to something else, or assigning it to someone in People, ends it too. The **With Student** status can't be renamed, because the helpdesk sets it itself.

Find these devices with **Held by → With a student** on the asset list, by searching for the student's name, or in the **With students** table on the asset report. An import can give them out too: see [Importing assets](import-assets).

## Tickets, comments and history

- **Tickets** - every ticket the asset was linked to. **Link a ticket** adds an existing one; **New ticket** logs a new one for it.
- **Comments** - notes about the asset, such as *Screen hinge loose*.
- **History** - every change, by whom and when.
