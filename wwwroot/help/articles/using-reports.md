**Reports** in the top bar is a menu of the reports your role can read, with the compliance registers under them. The tabs across a report are the others you can open; each report has its own tick on the role, so a business manager can be given finance and spend without seeing ticket workload, say.

| Report | What it answers |
| --- | --- |
| [Asset reports](asset-report) | What's due for review, what the fleet looks like, which devices cause most trouble |
| [Ticket reports](ticket-report) | Whether deadlines are met, how work is shared, what keeps coming back |
| [Parts and loans](parts-and-loans-reports) | What's low in stock, who borrows and why |
| [Finance and audit](finance-report) | What the school owns, what it cost, what arrived and left |
| [Projects](projects-report) | Where purchasing projects are, turnaround, spend by outcome |
| [Onboarding](onboarding-report) | New starters and which tasks run late |
| [Job spend](spend-report) | What site jobs cost, by budget code |
| [Premises checks](premises-checks-report) | The compliance log of statutory checks |
| [Cleaning rounds](rounds-report) | Rooms missed and spot-check scores |

## Choosing what's included

Most reports have a **period** or **academic year** at the top, and with more than one desk, a **desk**. Press **Apply** to update it, or **Reset** / **This year** to go back. Figures for things "now" (open tickets, overdue loans) are as they stand today, whatever the period.

The academic year starts in the month set in **Settings → Asset, part & loan rules**.

## Exporting and printing

With the *Export and print reports* tick on your role:

- **Print / Save as PDF** opens a print-ready version with the school's logo - choose *Save as PDF* in the print dialog to keep a copy for governors or the Trust.
- **CSV** links download the rows behind a section as a spreadsheet.

Reports only count tickets you're allowed to see, so with desks kept apart, each desk's lead sees their own desk's figures unless they can see all desks.
