When an asset leaves the school, **record a disposal** rather than deleting it. The record stays for finance and the auditors, and shows in the finance and audit report.

## Recording a disposal

1. If it's on loan or in a kit, book it back in or take it out of the kit first.
2. Open the asset and choose **Ownership & loans**.
3. Open **Record a disposal** and fill in:
   the **date**, **how** (sold, recycled, donated, written off...), any **proceeds**, who it was **disposed of by** (the recycling company, say), and a **certificate** number if you were given one.
4. Press **Record disposal**.

The asset then shows as **Disposed**: it's left out of the register unless you choose *Disposed* under **Show**, and out of loans and kits.

## Deleting instead

Delete an asset only if it was added by mistake. A deleted asset is gone from the register and its history, and from the reports.
