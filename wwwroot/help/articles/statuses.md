A **status** says where a ticket is: *Open*, *In progress*, *On Hold*, *Closed* and any others your school adds.

## Descriptions

Give each status a short **description**, such as *Waiting on the requester, a part, or a third party*. It's shown when someone picks a status, so everyone uses them the same way.

## Stopping the SLA clock

A status can **stop the SLA clock**, for tickets waiting on someone else - the requester, a part, a supplier. Press **Stop the SLA clock in this status** beside it (or **Let the SLA clock run** to undo).

While a ticket is in that status:

- it isn't counted as overdue or due soon, and shows **SLA paused**,
- when it moves on, its due date moves on by the time it was paused, counted in the SLA's own time (school days or lessons for those SLAs),
- a due date typed by hand is left as it is.

## Statuses kept to one desk

A status is used on every desk unless it's kept to one, such as *Awaiting contractor* for the Site desk. Choose the desk under **Used on**. Only that desk's tickets can be put in it, and a ticket moved to another desk goes back to that desk's first status.

## Renaming and removing

Renaming a status renames it on every ticket. A status that tickets use can't be deleted, and there must always be at least one open status for new tickets. **Closed** is how EduHelpdesk knows a ticket is finished, so it can't be renamed or removed - only its description can change.
