The **staff portal** (`/Portal`) is where staff who aren't helpdesk technicians - teachers, office staff, leaders - report problems, follow their tickets, and request purchasing projects. It's deliberately simple: no SLAs, teams or asset pickers, and staff never see internal notes or anyone else's tickets.

## Who can use it

- **Requesters** - anyone in **People → Users** with a portal password. Give one with **Give them a temporary password** on their record; they choose their own when they first sign in. Their **quick start guide** tells them where to go.
- **Helpdesk staff** - press **Open staff portal** in the menu under your name, or **Continue as** on the portal, to use it as yourself without a second password.

## What staff can do

- **Report a problem** - pick the desk (when there's more than one), the category tile, and the closest button from the service catalogue, or **Something else**. See [The service catalogue](service-catalogue).
- **My tickets** - their tickets, with replies; they can reply and add photos, and a reply within a few days of closing reopens the ticket.
- **Notifications** - a ping when a technician ticks **Notify the requester** on a reply.
- **Projects** - for people with **Can raise projects** or **Project lead** ticked on their record. See [How purchasing projects work](how-projects-work).

## Their help page

The portal has its own short help, in plain language, from the **?** in its top bar: [open the portal help](/Portal/Help). It covers signing in, reporting a problem, what's urgent, following a ticket, replying, notifications, projects and passwords.

> **Tip:** Add a note from your school to this article (below) and it's also shown at the top of the portal's help page - the best place for *For anything urgent, ring the IT office on 214* or the site team's radio channel.
