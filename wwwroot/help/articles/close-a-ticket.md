## Closing one ticket

1. Open the ticket.
2. Press **Mark as closed** in the bar at the top.
3. If the ticket asks for a **closing message**, write how the problem was fixed and press **Close ticket**. The requester sees it in the staff portal, so write it for them.

You can also close a ticket by choosing *Closed* as the status when you add a reply.

## When a closing message is needed

Your school chooses which priorities and categories need a closing message, in **Settings → Ticket queues & closing**. For example, every *High* priority ticket, so there's always a record of how it was fixed.

## Tickets that can't be closed yet

A **premises check** can't be closed until every line of its checklist is filled in. See [Doing a premises check](premises-checks).

## Closing many tickets at once

On the Tickets page, tick the tickets and choose **Close with a message**. Tickets that need a closing message are skipped if you leave it blank. See [Updating many tickets at once](update-many-tickets).

## If the requester replies after it's closed

If the requester replies in the staff portal within a few days of it closing, the ticket **reopens** by itself and shows a **Reply** badge, so nothing gets lost. How many days is set in **Settings → Ticket queues & closing**. After that, their reply starts a new conversation instead.
