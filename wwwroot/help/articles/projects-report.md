Choose the **period** (an academic year or all time). Open projects, workload and anything awaiting a decision are as they stand today; the period covers projects raised, proposals made and projects closed in it. Values are the **chosen quotes over the whole contract, including VAT**.

## The sections

- **Where projects are** - by status, with how many are overdue or due soon and the value of their chosen quotes, and each open project with its requester, technician, priority and needed-by date.
- **Technician workload** - active projects by priority today (as the project lead sees them when assigning), plus proposals made, median days to proposal and closures in the period.
- **Turnaround** - proposals first marked ready in the period: days from being raised, and how many were late against their needed-by date.
- **Spend by outcome** - projects closed in the period at their chosen quotes. *Approved* is what was agreed to be spent; *not approved* and *cancelled* show what was asked for and didn't go ahead.
