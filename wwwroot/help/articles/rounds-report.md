Choose the days (**From** and **To**, the last 28 days to start with) and a **round**, or every round.

- **The figures at the top** - rooms done of rooms due on recorded days, rooms missed, days not recorded, and the spot-check average.
- **By round** - days due, recorded and not recorded, rooms done and missed, the percentage done, and the average spot-check score.
- **Rooms missed** - each room missed at least once, how often, when last, and the notes left.
- **Spot-check scores** - average, number of checks and lowest score per room, lowest average first.
- **Days nothing was recorded** - usually holidays and training days, since the helpdesk doesn't hold term dates. A school day here means the round wasn't ticked off.

A room counts as missed only on a day that was recorded. **Print / Save as PDF** gives a copy for the business manager or the cleaning contractor; **CSV of every room** downloads one row per room per day.
