## Starting an onboarding

Press **New onboarding** and fill in who is starting:

- **Name**, **school email** (optional - often one of the tasks), **job title**, **department**, **location** (their main room or office) and **line manager**.
- **Start date** - task due dates are worked out from it.
- **Technician leading the IT side** - optional.
- **Checklist** - the template for their kind of role, from **Settings → Onboarding checklists**. Tasks can be added or removed for this person afterwards.

The new starter is added to **People**, with no way to sign in until their portal account is made.

## Tasks

![An onboarding](onboarding-page.png)

The top of the page shows how many tasks are done. Tasks are grouped into **stages** - *Before arrival*, *First day*, *First week* - each with a count, and a warning when one is overdue. When every task is done, the onboarding's ticket closes; unticking a task opens it again.

Each task has an owner and a due date. Tick it when it's done; untick it to put it back on the list. **Add a task** adds one just for this person, with how many **days from start** it's due and **who** does it.

## Tasks that do the job for you

Some tasks act when ticked:

- **Make their staff portal account** - gives them a temporary password and opens their quick start guide to print.
- **Issue a laptop** (or any asset type) - picks a free device from the asset register and assigns it to them (**Issue to...**). **Take it back** returns the device if it was issued by mistake.
- **Records their access** to a system - adds it to the access control register.

## Details

**Edit details** changes their details. Changing the **start date** moves the task due dates with it.

## Welcome pack

**Download welcome pack (PDF)** - one PDF with how to sign in, the equipment issued with a signature block, the school's IT information, and the documents the template includes (the acceptable use policy, say). For an hour after their password is made, it includes it; after that it doesn't. Untick documents under **Documents in their pack** to leave them out for this person.

## Notes, history, cancelling

**Notes** and **History** are kept on the onboarding's ticket. If they won't be starting, **They won't be starting → Cancel onboarding** with an optional reason (kept in the history); **Resume** if they're starting after all. **Delete onboarding** removes it completely.
