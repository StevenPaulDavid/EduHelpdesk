## Searching

Open the help from the **?** in the top bar (or **Help** in the menu under your name) and type what you're trying to do, such as *close a ticket* or *SLA*. Results show the best matches first.

Articles are grouped by area. There are two kinds:

- **How to** - step-by-step instructions for one job.
- **Page guide** - what every part of one page is for.

You can read every article, even about parts of EduHelpdesk your role can't open. Those are marked **Needs permission** - ask an administrator if you need that part.

## Help for the page you're on

When a page has a guide, there's a **? Help** link next to its title. It opens the guide for that page.

## Tips on fields

A small **?** beside a field explains what to put in it, usually with an example. Click or tap it to open the explanation, and click **More in help** for the full article. Press Esc or click anywhere else to close it.

## The school's own notes

Your school can add its own articles (under **School articles**) and a note at the top of any article, marked **From your school** - for example who to ring for a leak, or how your school does something. They're kept when EduHelpdesk is updated.

## What's new

**What's new** on the help page lists what changed in each version of EduHelpdesk.
