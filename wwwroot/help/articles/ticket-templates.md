A **ticket template** fills in a new ticket for a job that comes up again and again - a new starter's laptop, a room move, a password reset.

## What a template holds

**Name**, **type**, **category**, **priority**, **SLA**, **ticket title**, **description**, and defaults for any custom fields that apply to its category. Everything can still be changed on the ticket.

## Using one

On the New ticket form, choose it under **Start from a template**. See [Logging a new ticket](new-ticket).

## Offering it in the staff portal

Tick **Show in the staff portal** and staff see the template as a button under its category, next to the service catalogue items:

- its **name** is the button,
- the ticket's title is the template's **ticket title** (or its name if that's blank),
- staff can edit the description, but not the title,
- the **helper line** shows under the button.

Its place among the buttons is set on the service catalogue page. See [The service catalogue](service-catalogue).
