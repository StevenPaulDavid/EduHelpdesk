A **desk** is a part of the school the helpdesk serves, such as **IT**, **Site** and **Cleaning**. They share one EduHelpdesk, but each desk has its own categories, teams, people and ticket numbers. Desks are set up in **Settings → Desks**.

If your school has only one desk, you won't see desks anywhere.

## Which desk a ticket is on

A ticket is on the desk its **category** belongs to. *Heating* might be a Site category, *Printer* an IT one. In the staff portal, staff pick the desk first when they report a problem.

## Ticket numbers

Each desk counts its own tickets and has its own **prefix**, so IT-1042 and SITE-14 are plainly different teams' jobs. A desk can also have no prefix (plain #1042); only one desk can.

## Who sees what

You see the tickets of the desks you're on (set on your staff account). Other desks' tickets aren't shown to you anywhere - not in lists, search or reports - unless your role has **See all desks**, which is meant for leads such as the site manager or the IT manager.

Alerts follow desks too: pings, the bell, the Teams channel and EduNotify pings go to the people on the ticket's desk.

## Moving a ticket to another desk

Anyone can hand a ticket over. Give it one of the other desk's **categories** (click the pencil beside Category on the ticket):

- it takes the new desk's next ticket number, and its old number is kept in its history,
- a team, technician or status that belongs only to the old desk is cleared,
- you may no longer see it afterwards, if you're not on the new desk.

## Working hours

Each desk can count its SLAs in the school day or in **hours of its own**, set on the desk in **Settings → Desks**. With its own hours, minute and hour SLAs count only inside them: a spill reported at 17:55 with a 30-minute SLA is due at 06:25 the next working morning, not 18:25. Tickets already logged keep their due dates. See [Due dates and SLAs](due-dates-and-slas).

## Plant

Each **asset type** belongs to a desk, so boilers and fire extinguishers are the Site desk's and stay out of IT's asset lists.
