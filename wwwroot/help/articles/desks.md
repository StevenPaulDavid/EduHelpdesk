A **desk** is a part of the school the helpdesk serves. They share one EduHelpdesk, but each desk has its own categories, teams, people and ticket numbers. Desks are set up in **Settings → Desks**.

Two desks are **built in**:

- **IT** - devices, accounts, the network and classroom tech.
- **Facilities** - the site team's repairs, premises checks and cleaning. It starts with categories such as *Heating & hot water*, *Plumbing & leaks* and *Cleaning*, a staff portal button for each, the **Site team**, and the **FAC** prefix. While someone works on it, the top bar has a **Facilities** menu: see [Facilities](facilities).

Either can be **renamed** (call Facilities *Site* or *Estates* if that's what your school says) or **switched off** - an IT-only school switches Facilities off, a premises-only one IT. Switching a desk off sets its categories, teams and setup aside until it's switched back on; it can only be done while the desk has no tickets. One desk always stays on, and the built-in desks can't be deleted. You can also **add desks of your own**, such as Reprographics or a separate Cleaning desk.

If your school has only one desk on, you won't see desks anywhere.

An install from before Facilities was built in is asked once, in **Settings → Desks**, whether one of its own desks (Site, say) is the Facilities desk, to add one, or not now.

## The desk switcher

If you can see more than one desk, the top bar has a **desk switcher** next to the school's name. It says which desk you're working on: **My desks** (every desk you're on, the default), one desk such as **Site**, or **All desks** if your role sees more desks than your own.

The ticket list, the asset list and report, the daily sheet and the overview open on the desk you choose. It's remembered on your account, so it follows you to any computer. Opening a ticket or a link for another desk doesn't change it, and the desk filter on the ticket list still shows any desks you like.

## The top bar

Each desk chooses which parts of the top bar it uses, under **In the top bar** on the desk in **Settings → Desks**. The Facilities desk might use Assets and the Facilities menu, but not Kits, Loans or Projects. The bar shows a part when any desk you're working on uses it, so **My desks** and **All desks** show everything your desks use. **Tickets**, **Reports** and **Settings** are always there.

This only tidies the bar. Nobody gets a page their role can't open, and an unticked part can still be reached by a link.

## Which desk a ticket is on

A ticket is on the desk its **category** belongs to. *Heating & hot water* is a Facilities category, *Printer* an IT one. In the staff portal, staff pick the desk first when they report a problem.

## Ticket numbers

Each desk counts its own tickets and has its own **prefix**, so IT-1042 and FAC-14 are plainly different teams' jobs. A desk can also have no prefix (plain #1042); only one desk can.

## Who sees what

You see the tickets of the desks you're on (set on your technician account). Other desks' tickets aren't shown to you anywhere - not in lists, search or reports - unless your role has **See all desks**, which is meant for leads such as the site manager or the IT manager.

Alerts follow desks too: pings, the bell, the Teams channel and EduNotify pings go to the people on the ticket's desk.

## Moving a ticket to another desk

Anyone can hand a ticket over. Give it one of the other desk's **categories** (click the pencil beside Category on the ticket):

- it takes the new desk's next ticket number, and its old number is kept in its history,
- a team, technician or status that belongs only to the old desk is cleared,
- you may no longer see it afterwards, if you're not on the new desk.

## Working hours

Each desk can count its SLAs in the school day or in **hours of its own**, set on the desk in **Settings → Desks**. With its own hours, minute and hour SLAs count only inside them: a spill reported at 17:55 with a 30-minute SLA is due at 06:25 the next working morning, not 18:25. Tickets already logged keep their due dates. See [Due dates and SLAs](due-dates-and-slas).

## Plant

Each **asset type** belongs to a desk, so boilers and fire extinguishers are the Facilities desk's and stay out of IT's asset lists.
