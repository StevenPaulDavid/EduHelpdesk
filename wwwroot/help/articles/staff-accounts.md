A **technician account** is a helpdesk login: IT technicians, site staff, cleaning supervisors, the business manager. (People who only report problems are **requesters** and use the staff portal instead.) Technician accounts are on the **Technicians** tab of the **People** page.

## Adding one

- **Name** and **Email** - they sign in with the email.
- **Team** - the team they're in. Tickets assigned to a team can be given to its people.
- **Desks** - which desks they work on, when there's more than one. They see only those desks' tickets, unless their role has *See all desks*. See [Desks: IT, Site and Cleaning](desks).
- **Role** - what they can see and do. See [Roles and permissions](roles-and-permissions).
- **Active account?** - switch it off when they leave; their name stays on everything they did.
- **Password** - leave it blank and EduHelpdesk makes up a **temporary password**, which they change the first time they sign in. A password you type is theirs to keep.

## Signing in

The account page says whether they have a temporary password. Their **quick start guide** tells them where to go and how to start; printed within an hour of setting a password, it includes it. **Give them a temporary password** makes a new one if they've forgotten theirs.

## When something's lost

- **Recovery key** - if they've lost it, or someone else may have seen it, **Remove recovery key**; they can make a new one.
- **Two-step sign-in** - if they've lost their phone and their recovery codes, **Reset two-step sign-in**. They then sign in with just their password, and set it up again (straight away, if it's required).

## Many at once

Import a spreadsheet of technician accounts in **Settings → Imports**. See [Importing from spreadsheets](imports).
