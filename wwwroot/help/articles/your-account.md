Everything about your own login is in the menu under **your name** in the top bar.

## Change password

Enter your current password and a new one twice. A good password is three random words together: easy to remember, hard to guess. If you were given a temporary password, you're asked to choose your own the first time you sign in.

## Two-step sign-in

After your password, the helpdesk asks for a code from an app on your phone (Microsoft Authenticator, Google Authenticator or similar), so a password on its own isn't enough to get in.

1. Open **Two-step sign-in** and scan the code with the app (or type the key shown).
2. Enter the code the app shows and press **Turn on two-step sign-in**.
3. **Print** or save your **recovery codes**. Each one works once, if you haven't got your phone.

New phone? Ask an administrator to reset your two-step sign-in, then set it up again. Your school may require it for everyone.

## Recovery key

If you forget your password, a **recovery key** lets you set a new one yourself from the sign-in page, without waiting for an administrator. It works once. Make one while you know your password, and keep the file somewhere safe. (Your school may have turned recovery keys off.)

## Notifications

**Notifications** lists new tickets and replies from the last 14 days, and is where you switch on **pings and pop-ups** for this browser. The **bell** in the top bar shows the same list, with a count of unread ones; **Mark all as read** clears it.

## Signing out

**Sign out** at the bottom of the menu ends your session, so leave nothing signed in on a shared computer.
