**Assets → Parts** lists spares and consumables in stock - toner, cables, keyboards, light fittings - where they're kept and who supplies them.

## Adding a part

Only **Name** is required. The rest helps:

- **SKU / part number** - the supplier's code.
- **Category** and **Location / bin** - from the lists in Settings.
- **Quantity on hand** - how many you have now.
- **Reorder threshold** - flag it as low once the quantity drops to this or below. Leave blank to use the default in **Settings → Asset, part & loan rules**.
- **Suppliers** - who you buy it from.
- **For asset types** - which kinds of asset it fits, so it's easier to find when fixing one.

## Changing the stock

On the part's page:

- **Add to stock** - when a delivery arrives, enter how many and an optional note.
- **Count wrong? Set the exact quantity instead** - for a stocktake, breakage or anything gone missing. It replaces the count rather than adding to it, and asks for a reason.

Every change is listed with who made it and when.

## Using a part on a job

On a ticket's **Parts** tab, **Use a part** takes it out of stock and records it against the job. Deleting the ticket puts the parts back.

## Low stock

Choose **Low stock only** under **Show** to see what needs ordering. The parts report lists them too.

Tick parts in the list to **change category** or **location** for several at once.
