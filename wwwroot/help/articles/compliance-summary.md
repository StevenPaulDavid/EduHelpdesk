The DfE digital and technology standards ask schools to keep three registers: **assets**, **contracts** and **access control**. **Reports → Compliance summary** shows all three, and lists everything on them that needs acting on, most urgent first.

![The compliance summary](compliance-summary.png)

## The figures at the top

- **To act on now** - overdue, ended, unapproved or leavers: an overdue asset check, a contract past its notice date, privileged access nobody approved, a leaver who still has access.
- **Coming up** - things due soon or ending soon.
- How many assets are in use and how many contracts are live.

## Each register

Each register's card shows how many records it holds and gives:

- **Excel (DfE layout)** - the register in the layout of the DfE template, for governors, auditors or the Trust.
- **CSV** - a plain spreadsheet.
- **Import** - bring records in from the DfE template or a spreadsheet. Nothing changes until you've seen the preview and pressed Apply; importing the same file again updates rather than duplicates, and blank cells never clear anything. For access control, **Import a staff list** adds the people first.

## What needs acting on

The list under the cards is every issue across the registers, with what's wrong and a link to the record. Choose a register above it to see just that one.

## Reminders

Once a day, anything **newly** due or overdue sends a reminder under the **bell** to everyone who can open that register. Each issue reminds once, not every morning; if it's fixed and falls due again, it reminds again.

You see the registers your role can open. See [The asset register](asset-register), [The contracts register](contracts-register) and [The access control register](access-register).
