When staff report a problem in the staff portal, they tap a **category tile**, then one of the **buttons** under it. The button becomes the ticket's title. If theirs isn't listed, they tap **Something else** and type it, so the catalogue never stops anyone reporting a problem. With more than one desk, they pick the desk first.

## Category tiles

The big tiles on the first screen, one per category, in this order. For each, pick an **icon** and a **colour**, and use the arrows to move it up or down. The order also applies to category lists elsewhere. Categories themselves are added and renamed in **Settings → Categories**.

## Buttons

Add an item under **Add an item**:

- **Category** - the tile it sits under.
- **Name** - the button, and the ticket's title, such as *Projector not working*.
- **Starting priority** - what the portal's priority box starts on for this button; staff can still change it. Leave blank to start on *Normal*.
- **Helper line** - a few words under the button, such as *No picture, or flickering*.
- **Show in the staff portal** - untick to keep it in the catalogue without offering it to staff.

Use the arrows to put the buttons in order, most common first.

> **Tip:** Keep it short. Six to ten buttons per tile is plenty; *Something else* catches the rest.
