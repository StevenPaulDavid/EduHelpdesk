**Settings** holds everything that shapes how EduHelpdesk works for your school. Changes affect everyone, so most of it needs **Settings: Edit** on your role.

![The Settings page](settings.png)

## Finding a setting

Type in **Find a setting** at the top - *SLA*, *logo*, *backup*, *cleaning* - and the cards narrow to the matching pages. Each card says what the page is for and, for lists, how many items it holds. Once inside, the menu down the side takes you to any other settings page.

## How it's organised

| Section | What's in it |
| --- | --- |
| Tickets | Desks, statuses, categories, priorities, SLAs, the school day, the service catalogue, templates, recurring tickets, custom fields, budget codes |
| Assets & inventory | Asset types, makes, models, statuses and rules, parts and loans |
| People & places | Teams, departments, buildings, rooms, cleaning rounds and cleaners, imports, onboarding checklists |
| Compliance | Contract and access register lists |
| Projects | Spending bands and purchasing requirements |
| System | Branding, backups, sign-in security, notifications, Teams, EduNotify, data retention, the audit log, the database and error log, going live |

**About EduHelpdesk** shows the version installed. See [What's new](/Help/WhatsNew) for what changed in it.

## Who can open what

- **Settings: Edit** on a role opens the Settings pages and lets it change them.
- The **audit log** and the **raw database** have permissions of their own, so a data protection officer can be given those without being able to change settings. They then appear in the top menu instead of Settings.
- **Administrator** can always do everything.

See [Roles and permissions](roles-and-permissions).
