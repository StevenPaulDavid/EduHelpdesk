Open a ticket by clicking it in the ticket list. The page has four parts: the bar at the top, the description and details, the tabs, and the side panel.

![A ticket](ticket-page.png)

## The bar at the top

The bar stays at the top of the screen as you scroll. It shows the ticket's number and title, its status, priority, how long until it's due, and who reported it. Its buttons:

- **Assign to me** - take the job.
- **On my way** - take the job and tell the requester you're coming. They get a pop-up in the staff portal naming you.
- **Mark as closed** - close it. Some tickets ask for a closing message first. See [Closing a ticket](close-a-ticket).
- **Print** - a printed **Ticket**, a **Label** to stick on a device, a **Job sheet** to take with you, or the whole ticket as a PDF: **Full report** (everything, internal notes included) or **Requester copy** (without internal notes or history, to give to someone outside the team).
- **More** - **Merge into another ticket** and **Delete ticket**. These need permission to delete tickets.

## Description and details

**Edit details** changes the title and description.

If the ticket is a premises check, its **checklist** is here. See [Doing a premises check](premises-checks).

Under **Assignment & scheduling**, click the pencil beside a field to change it:

- **Assigned technician** - who's doing it. The list shows the people in the ticket's team.
- **Assigned team** - the group responsible.
- **SLA** - how long the ticket has. See [Due dates and SLAs](due-dates-and-slas).
- **Due date** - when it's due. Changing it by hand marks it as an *Override*.

## The tabs

- **Comments** - the reply box and the conversation, newest first. See [Replies and internal notes](replies-and-internal-notes).
- **History** - every change to the ticket: what, when and who. It can't be edited.
- **Assets** - the devices or equipment the job is about. **Link assets** to add more.
- **Parts** - parts used from stock. **Use a part** takes it out of stock.
- **Costs** - what the job cost, if your role can see costs. See [Recording what a job cost](job-costs).
- **Attachments** - photos and documents. **Attach files** to add some.

## The side panel

- **Requester** - who reported it, with their email, department and room.
- **Classification** - status, priority, type, category and location, each with a pencil to change it. With more than one desk it also shows the **desk**. See [Desks: IT, Site and Cleaning](desks).
- **Related tickets** - **Link or follow up** links another ticket, or creates a follow-up ticket from this one. See [Linking, merging and follow-ups](link-merge-and-follow-up).
- **Purchasing projects** - start a purchasing project from this ticket, or link it to one.
- **Staff notice** - **Tell staff** puts an outage message on staff PCs through EduNotify, if your school uses it and your role allows. It's taken down automatically when the ticket closes, or with **Recall**.
- **Additional details** - any extra fields your school has added for this category.
