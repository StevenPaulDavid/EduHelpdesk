Each new member of staff gets **one onboarding**: a checklist of everything that needs doing before and after they start - an account, a laptop, a door fob, training - with each task owned by the person or team who does it.

## Starting an onboarding

1. Open **Onboarding** (under **HR** if you also have People).
2. Add the new starter's details and start date, and pick the **Checklist** for their kind of role (teacher, support staff, and so on). The checklists are set up in **Settings → Onboarding checklists**.
3. The tasks are created from the template, each with its owner and when it's due relative to the start date.

## Following progress

The onboarding list shows how far each new starter has got, and which tasks are late. Each owner sees their own tasks: IT staff find theirs under **Tickets → Onboarding**.

## The welcome pack

When the onboarding is ready, open it and use **Download welcome pack (PDF)**: the new starter's first-day information and the school's welcome documents in one PDF.
