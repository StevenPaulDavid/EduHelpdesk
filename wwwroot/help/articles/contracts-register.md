The **contracts register** is the DfE's register of what the school pays suppliers for: subscriptions, licences, support, broadband, leases - anything with dates to keep an eye on. Open it from **Compliance → Contracts register**.

## The list

The figures at the top give the yearly cost of live contracts, renewals in the next 3 months, and notice dates in the next month. **Show** picks out contracts that **need attention**, are **renewing soon**, have a **notice date** coming up, are **ending**, **live** or **expired**, and **approved applications** or those that **process personal data**. **Export CSV** downloads the list.

## Adding a contract

Press **Add contract**. Only the name is required, but the more you fill in, the more the register can warn you about.

| Section | What to put |
| --- | --- |
| The contract or service | Its **name**, **description**, **contract type**, **spend category**, **status**, the **contract owner** in school, and the **procurement approach** used |
| Supplier | From the supplier directory, and the supplier's contact |
| Cost | The **cost** and what it covers (per month, quarter or year, the total for the contract, or one-off), and any cost notes |
| Dates and renewal | **Start**, **end** and **next renewal** dates, the **notice needed** in months, the duration and how it renews |
| Compliance | Whether it's an **approved application**, **processes personal data**, or is a **related party transaction** |

**Notice needed** counts back from the renewal date (or the end date): with 3 months' notice on a 1 September renewal, the notice date is 1 June. That's the date the register warns about, because missing it usually means another year's contract.

A contract can also be added from a purchasing project's chosen quote, filled in for you. **Import** brings in the DfE template, filled in, or this register's own export.

## Compliance ticks

- **Approved application** - on the cyber security standard's list of approved apps.
- **Processes personal data** - needs a DPIA and a line on the information asset register.
- **Related party transaction** - under the Academy Trust Handbook, report it to the DfE before it starts or renews, and record when (**Reported to DfE on**).

## A contract's page

Shows the cost, renewal and notice details, the **assets** leased or loaned under it (linked from each asset's Purchase & warranty tab), and its history. When a contract ends, set its status to **Expired** to keep the record; **Remove from the register** deletes it for good.
