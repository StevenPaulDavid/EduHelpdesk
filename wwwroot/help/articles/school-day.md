SLAs measured in **work days** or **periods** count against this page, so a ticket logged on Friday afternoon isn't overdue by Sunday.

## School days

Tick the days the school is open. Work-day and period SLAs skip any day that isn't ticked. Recurring tickets set to *move to the next school day* use them too.

> **Note:** Holidays and INSET days aren't taken into account yet, so tickets logged just before a break will show as overdue over it.

## Periods

List the lessons with their start and end times. The same timings are used on every school day.

- Leave gaps for break, lunch and after school - they don't count.
- A period SLA starts counting from the **next period to begin**, so the one already under way when a ticket is logged doesn't count. *1 period* means by the end of the next full lesson.

**Add another period** adds a row; the bin removes one. Press **Save periods**. The page lists which SLAs use periods. Tickets already logged keep the due dates they have.

## Trying it out

**If a ticket were logged now** shows when a ticket logged at this moment would be due under each work-day and period SLA, using the saved settings - a quick way to check the timetable is right.

Desks with their own working hours (a site team's early and late shifts) are set on the desk instead. See [Desks: IT, Site and Cleaning](desks#working-hours).
