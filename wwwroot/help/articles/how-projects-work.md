A **purchasing project** is a request to source and quote for something - a class set of iPads, a new projector, a lease. IT gathers quotes and produces a **proposal**; someone else decides.

## The steps

| Status | What's happening |
| --- | --- |
| **New** | Raised in the staff portal, waiting for the project lead to assign it |
| **Gathering quotes** | A technician is splitting it into items, asking suppliers and collecting quotes |
| **Proposal ready** | The proposal PDF is done; the requester can download it in the portal |
| **Closed** | Decided - **Approved**, **Not approved** or **Cancelled**, with an optional note |

## Who does what

- **Line managers and SLT** raise projects from the **staff portal**, if their People record has **Can raise projects** ticked. They give a title, when the proposal is **needed by**, the items wanted, any purchasing requirements (such as a lease option) and a **suggested priority**.
- The **project lead** (a People record with **Project lead** ticked) sees every project in the portal, confirms the **priority** and **assigns** a technician, choosing by each technician's workload. Assigning moves it to *Gathering quotes*.
- The **technician** works it in the helpdesk: items, suppliers, quotes, prices, and the proposal. See [The project page](project-page) and [Quotes and prices](quotes-and-prices).
- Whoever decides closes it with the **outcome**.

Project priorities run from **P1 · Urgent** (teaching, safety or statutory work is affected now) to **P5 · When possible**, separate from ticket priorities.

## What the requester sees

In the portal, the requester sees their own projects: the status, the technician, the needed-by date, the tidied list of items and shared notes. They can add notes, and download the proposal once it's **Proposal ready**. Quotes and prices aren't shown in the portal.

## Starting from a ticket

When a ticket turns out to be a purchase ("we need six more laptops"), use **Start a project** in the ticket's side panel. The project is raised for the ticket's requester and waits for the lead, linked to the ticket.

## Reopening

A closed project can be **reopened** if it comes back; it returns to *Gathering quotes* and its outcome is cleared.
