**Tickets** in the top menu lists every ticket you can work on. Click any row to open the ticket.

![The ticket list](tickets-list.png)

## The buttons at the top

- **Daily sheet** - open jobs by room, ready to print and hand out. See [The daily sheet](daily-sheet).
- **Log a job already done** - for something dealt with before anyone logged it. See [Logging a job already done](quick-log).
- **New ticket** - log a job that still needs doing. See [Logging a new ticket](new-ticket).

## Queues

The tabs are the queues. The number on each tab is how many tickets are in it.

| Tab | What's in it |
| --- | --- |
| Open | Every ticket that isn't closed |
| My tickets | Open tickets assigned to you, and onboardings with an IT task given to you |
| Unassigned | Open tickets with no technician yet |
| Overdue / due soon | Open tickets past their due date, or due within the window set in Settings |
| Onboarding | New starters' onboardings with IT tasks still to do |
| All tickets | Everything, closed tickets included |

## Working as

**Working as** says whose tickets **My tickets** shows. Normally it's you. If your role has *Change "Working as"*, you can pick a colleague - handy for covering someone's queue while they're off. The choice is remembered in this browser only.

## Searching and filtering

Type in the search box to look through ticket numbers, titles, descriptions, comments, requesters, asset tags and technicians. You can type a number with or without its prefix (IT-1042 or 1042).

**Filters** opens a panel to narrow the list by desk, technician, team, requester, department, location, status, priority, type and category, and to choose how many rows a page shows. Each filter in force shows as a chip under the search box; click a chip's **×** to remove it, or **Clear all**.

> **Tip:** With more than one desk, the list starts on your own desks. Tick desks in the Desk filter to see others (if your role allows), or tick none to see every desk you can.

Click a column heading to sort by it; click again to reverse.

## What the colours mean

- A **red bar** down the left of a row: the ticket is overdue.
- An **amber bar**: it's due soon.
- A **Reply** badge: the requester has replied in the staff portal and is waiting to hear back.

## Updating many tickets at once

Tick the tickets, choose an action under **With selected tickets**, and press **Apply to selected**. See [Updating many tickets at once](update-many-tickets).
