A **loan kit** is a named set of equipment lent out together - *Loan laptop 3* with its charger and mouse, say. **Assets → Kits** lists every kit, what's in it, whether it's available, and who has it.

The figures at the top show how many are **ready to issue**, **with staff now**, and **past their due-back date**.

## Adding a kit

Press **Add kit**:

- **Kit name** - what's written on the bag or label.
- **Notes** - anything worth knowing, such as *Charger has a UK plug only*.
- **Add an asset** - search for the assets in the kit and add them. Assets assigned to someone, on loan, disposed of or already in another kit aren't offered. Leave it empty if you don't track the contents.

## Swapping contents

Open the kit and remove or add assets. The kit keeps its loan history, so a laptop swapped for another doesn't lose the record.

## Issuing and returning

**Issue** beside a kit starts a loan with that kit; **Book back in** returns it. See [Loans](loans).

## Retiring a kit

Tick **Retired** to stop offering it for new loans while keeping its history. A kit with loan history can't be deleted, so the loan report keeps the full picture.
