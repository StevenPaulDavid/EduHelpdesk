How well tickets are being dealt with. Choose the **period**, the **ticket type** (incidents, requests or both) and the **desk**.

![The ticket report](ticket-report.png)

## The figures at the top

Tickets created in the period, how many closed, how many **met their due date**, the **median resolution time**, and how many are **overdue now**.

## SLA performance

By priority, category and team: tickets **on time** (closed by their due date), **late** (closed after it), **open and overdue**, and with **no due date**. The **on-time rate** leaves out tickets that are open but not yet due, and those with no due date. The worst-performing rows come first. **Average resolution** runs from creation to closing. Below it, the open tickets furthest past their due date.

## Technician workload

Per technician: open tickets now (incidents and requests), how many are overdue, the oldest open one, and how many they closed in the period. Tickets nobody has picked up are under **Unassigned**. **By team** totals the same per team, including tickets waiting for the team as a whole.

## What keeps coming back

- **Devices with repeat faults** - the same device with tickets in the same category more than once: a fix that didn't hold, or a device on its way out.
- **Assets** and **requesters with the most tickets**.
- **Categories** - tickets per category, compared with the same length of time just before the period.
