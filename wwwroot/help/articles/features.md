**Settings → Features** switches whole parts of the helpdesk off and on. A school that only wants tickets can switch off assets, loans, parts, projects and the rest, and the helpdesk is just that.

| Feature | What goes |
| --- | --- |
| Assets | The asset register, devices on tickets, the asset and finance reports, and the asset settings |
| Kits and loans | Lending devices and kits, and the loans report. Needs Assets |
| Parts | The parts inventory, parts used on tickets, and the parts report |
| Projects | Purchasing projects, the staff portal's project requests, and the projects report |
| Onboarding | New staff checklists and the onboarding report. Onboarding tickets become ordinary tickets |
| Compliance registers | The contracts register, the access control register and the compliance summary |
| Cleaning rounds | Rounds, the day's sheets, the cleaners list and the rounds report |
| Premises checks | Checklists on recurring tickets, the premises checks page and the yearly log. Schedules log plain tickets |
| Job costs | Costs on tickets, budget codes and the job spend report |

## What switching off does

- It's gone **for everyone**, Administrators included: from the top bar, the reports and the dashboard, tickets, the staff portal, people's pages, the role editor, Settings and this help. Its pages say it isn't used here.
- **Nothing is deleted.** Switching it back on brings back everything it had - records, settings and what each role was allowed to do with it.
- **Kits and loans need Assets**, so switching Assets off switches them off too, and switching them on switches Assets on.

Tickets, people, reports and settings are the helpdesk itself and are always there. The built-in IT and Facilities desks are switched off and on in [Desks](desks).
