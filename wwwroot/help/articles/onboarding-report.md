Choose the **period**. Onboardings still in progress are as they stand today; everything else covers people whose start date falls in the period. A task is **late** when it was ticked after its due date, or is past it and still not done; tasks not due yet aren't counted either way.

## The sections

- **Onboardings in progress** - each starter, their template, when they start, tasks done, IT tasks left, and anything overdue.
- **Tasks that run late** - the same task across different starters, matched by name, most often late first. A task late every time may need an earlier due date in its template.
- **Who the tasks fell to** - on time and overdue, by owner. IT tasks given to a named technician are theirs; the rest are the IT team's.
- **By kind of starter** - by template: started, finished, cancelled, and the median time to finish.
- **New starters** - everyone in the period, with their department, start date and status.
