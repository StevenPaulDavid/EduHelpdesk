The top of a project shows its title, number (PRJ-0012), when the proposal is **needed by** (marked *Due soon* or *Late*), and its actions.

![A purchasing project](project-page.png)

## The actions at the top

- **Proposal (PDF)** - the proposal to download. Before *Proposal ready* it's marked **Draft**. See *The proposal* below.
- **Assign** / **Reassign** - the project lead normally assigns from the staff portal; this is the helpdesk-side backup when they can't (needs *Assign projects*). It also confirms the **priority**.
- **Status** with **Set** - move it between *New*, *Gathering quotes* and *Proposal ready*.
- **Close project** - record the **outcome** (Approved, Not approved, Cancelled) and an optional note. **Reopen** brings a closed project back.
- **More → Delete project** - removes it for good, notes and history included. To keep the record, close it as *Cancelled* instead.

## Spending band

The boxes at the top show the project's **progress**, its **chosen total** (over the term and in the first year), its **spending band**, and how many **quotes** are in. Press the **i** in the Spending band box to see your finance policy's bands with this project's band highlighted - how many quotes it needs, anything else it must meet, and how many quotes each item has so far. See [Spending bands](spending-bands).

## The tabs

- **Overview** - what was asked for, the purchasing requirements, linked **tickets** (add one by number), and **Edit details** to change the title, needed-by date or items wanted.
- **Items & quotes** - the main things being bought, each with its suppliers and quotes. **Create items from the request** splits the request into items to start you off. See [Quotes and prices](quotes-and-prices).
- **Notes** - notes shared with the requester in the portal, or **internal notes** for the IT team only.
- **History** - every change, by whom and when.

## The proposal

One PDF, in this order: a front page, contents, a **cost table** for each item comparing every received quote with the chosen one marked, first-year and whole-term totals, and then every **PDF or picture quote** attached. Word and Excel quotes can't be merged, so they're listed with a note. The requester can download it in the portal once the project is **Proposal ready**.

## Record what was approved

Once a project is decided, each chosen quote can be **added to the contracts register**, filled in from the quote - worth doing for anything with renewals, a subscription or a lease. See [The contracts register](contracts-register).
