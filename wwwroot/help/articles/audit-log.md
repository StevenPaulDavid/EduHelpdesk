The **audit log** lists every change made in the helpdesk, newest first: settings, accounts, roles, people, assets, contracts, access grants and more, plus sign-ins and each look at the raw database.

## Reading it

Each line shows **who** made the change, the **area**, the **item**, the **action** (created, updated, deleted, added, removed) and the **details** - for an update, what each field was before and after.

Ticket, asset and project changes are also kept on the record itself, in its own history.

## Filtering

Narrow it by **area**, **action**, **who**, a **from** and **to** date, or search text. The heading says how many events match.

## Who can see it

The audit log has its own permission, so a data protection officer can be given it without being able to change settings. Old entries are removed after the period set in **Settings → Data retention**.
