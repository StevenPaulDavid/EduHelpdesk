The **planner** puts tickets on the days they're **planned for**, so you can see who has what on which day and share the week out. It uses each ticket's **Planned for** date: see [Planning a ticket for a day](plan-a-ticket).

Only the helpdesk sees the plan. Requesters never do, and planning a ticket doesn't change its due date or SLA.

## Opening it

On the **Tickets** page, press **Planner** in the **List | Board | Planner** switch beside the queues. The queue you're on, the search and the filters stay as they are, so the planner shows the same tickets the list would.

## Week and month

- **Week** - a row for each person on the desks being shown, with **Unassigned** at the top, and a column for each school day. Each row says how many tickets that person has planned.
- **Month** - an ordinary calendar. Each day shows up to four tickets, then **+ more**, which opens that day as a list. Click a day's number to open its week.

**← Previous**, **This week** (or **This month**) and **Next →** move through time. Today is shaded.

The columns are your school's days (**Settings → School day**). A day outside them, such as a Saturday, appears whenever something is planned for it.

## The side lists

- **Planned day gone by** - open tickets whose planned day has passed, whichever week you're looking at. Give each a new day, or close it.
- **Not planned** - open tickets with no day yet. This is where the week's work comes from.

Each shows up to 40 tickets, with **Open as a list** for the rest.

## Planning a ticket

You need permission to edit tickets.

- **Drag** a card onto a day. On the week, the row you drop it on is who it's given to: drop it in Sam's Thursday and it's planned for Thursday and assigned to Sam. On the month, only the day changes.
- Drag a card to **Not planned** to take it off the plan. It stays with whoever has it.
- Or press **Plan** on the card, choose the day and who, and press **Plan**; **Take off the plan** is there too. This works with a keyboard, and on a phone or tablet where dragging doesn't.

Each change is written to the ticket's History. If a move isn't allowed - the person isn't in the ticket's team, say - nothing changes and a message says why.

## What the cards show

The ticket's number and title (click to open it) and its priority, and:

- **Not done** - the planned day has gone by and the ticket is still open.
- **After it's due** - the planned day is later than the due date.
- A **red bar** means the ticket is overdue; **amber**, due soon.

Closed tickets are only on the planner in the **All tickets** queue, crossed through, as a record of when the work was planned.

> **Tip:** On Monday morning, open the planner on the **Open** queue: clear **Planned day gone by** first, then drag from **Not planned** into the week until each row looks fair.
