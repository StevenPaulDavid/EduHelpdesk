## Parts report

**Parts at or below their reorder threshold** - each part's category, location, suppliers, quantity and threshold, with how many are completely out of stock. Use it to restock before a technician is blocked. The default threshold is in **Settings → Asset, part & loan rules**; a part can have its own.

## Loan report

Who is borrowing devices, how often and why - kits and individual devices together. Choose the **period**, the **loan type** and a **reason**.

- **Repeat borrowers** - anyone who has taken a set number of devices in a set number of days (both set in Settings), with their reasons, so genuine cases (supply staff, a device in for repair) can be told apart from people repeatedly forgetting their own. Print it as a short list for a line manager.
- **Everyone who borrowed a device** - each person's loans, with what, why, when it was due and when it came back.
- **Reason breakdown** - loans by reason.
- **Currently on loan** - everything out now, and for how long.

Loans from before reasons were recorded show as *not recorded* and are left out of the repeat borrowers list.
