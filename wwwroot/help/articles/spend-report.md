Totals the **cost lines** recorded on tickets' Costs tabs. Choose the **academic year** and the **desk**. Lines count by the date on each cost line.

- **The figures at the top** - gross spend (net plus VAT, as typed), and how many jobs and cost lines it covers.
- **Where it went** - by **budget code**, **desk**, **month** and **supplier**.
- **Every cost line** - date, ticket, what it was for, supplier, budget code, net, VAT and gross, linking to each ticket.

See [Recording what a job cost](job-costs).
