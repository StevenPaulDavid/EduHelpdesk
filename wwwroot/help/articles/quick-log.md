Plenty of jobs are sorted on the spot: a call on the radio, a spill outside the hall, a door that won't shut. **Log a job already done** puts them on the record in a few seconds, so the work shows in the reports and the room's history.

Open it from the **Tickets** page: **More → Log a job already done**.

![Logging a job already done](quick-log.png)

## The form

- **What kind of job** - the category. With more than one desk they're grouped by desk.
- **What was dealt with** - a short line, such as *Spill cleaned up outside the hall*.
- **Where** - the room.
- **Who dealt with it** - only people on the job's desk are listed.
- **When** - when it was done. It starts at now; change it if you're catching up later.
- **Notes** - anything worth keeping: what was used, or something to follow up.

Press **Save**, or **Save and log another** to record the next one straight away.

## What happens to it

It's saved as a **closed ticket** with no due date, so it never shows as overdue, and **nobody is notified**. It counts in the ticket reports like any other job. If it turns out more work is needed, open it and use **Link or follow up** to start a follow-up ticket.
