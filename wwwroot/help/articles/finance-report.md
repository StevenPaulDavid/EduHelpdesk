What the school owns, what it cost, and what arrived and left. Choose the **academic year**; it's shown alongside the calendar year. Figures are what was spent - nothing is depreciated.

## The sections

- **Holdings** - assets still held, by type, location and so on, with their total cost. Rows note assets with no price recorded.
- **Additions** - assets bought in the year, counted by **purchase date** (not when they were entered), with price, reference and supplier.
- **Disposals** - assets disposed of in the year: how, any proceeds, and what they originally cost. See [Disposing of an asset](dispose-an-asset).
- **Spend by order** - grouped by the Trust's **purchase order** where there is one, otherwise the supplier's quote reference. It covers the assets recorded against the order, so it can be lower than an invoice that also included cables, licences or services.

The academic year's start month is set in **Settings → Asset, part & loan rules**.
