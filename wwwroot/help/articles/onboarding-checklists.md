**Settings → Onboarding checklists** holds one **template** for each kind of new starter - *Teacher*, *Support staff*, *Supply and cover*. Starting an onboarding copies a template's tasks, which can then be changed for that person.

## Adding a template

Give it a **name**, and choose **Start from** another template to copy its tasks and save typing.

## Tasks

Each task has:

- **Task** - what needs doing, such as *Create school email account*.
- **Days from start** - when it's due: **-5** is five days before their first day, **0** is their first day, **3** is three days after.
- **Who** - the person or team who does it. IT tasks show in the ticket list.
- **Does** - a task can do the job when it's ticked: **Makes their staff portal account** (a temporary password and a quick start guide to print), **Issues a laptop** or any asset type (picks a free device from the asset register and assigns it), or **Records their access to** a system on the access control register.

## What goes in every welcome pack

Each onboarding can print a **welcome pack**: their sign-in details, the equipment issued with a signature block, the **school IT information** below, then the PDFs each template chooses.

- **School IT information** - Wi-Fi, printing, who to ask for help, where to find policies. A blank line starts a new paragraph.
- **Documents** - upload PDFs (the acceptable use policy, the staff IT handbook), then tick which templates include them under each template's **Welcome pack documents**. Password-protected PDFs can't be added.
