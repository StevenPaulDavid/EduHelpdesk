**Reports → Dashboard** shows the helpdesk as charts, for the desks chosen in the desk switcher. Choose the period along the top: the last 7 days, 30 days, 3 months or 12 months. Charts over time step by the day for a month or less, by the week up to a few months, and by the month beyond.

Each part shows only when your role has that report ticked (**People → Roles**) and the desks you're working on use that area:

| Part | Needs | Charts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tickets | Ticket reports | Open tickets over time, logged and closed, workload by technician and priority, when open tickets are due, and the busiest days and times |
| Assets | Asset reports | Fleet by the year it was bought (past its expected life on top), warranties ending in the next 12 months, assets by status, and devices with repeat faults |
| Spend | Job spend report | Job costs each month by budget code, ex VAT |
| Loans | Loan reports | Kits and devices on loan, and overdue, over the period |
| Parts | Parts reports | Parts at or below their reorder level, with the line where to reorder |
| Projects | Project reports | Active projects at each stage, and how many are past their due date |

**Click a bar or a point** to open the list behind it: a technician's open tickets of one priority, the tickets logged on a day, the assets with a status, a device, a part. Hover over one, or tab to it, for its figures. **Show as table** under each chart gives the same numbers as a table.

The page **redraws itself every 5 minutes** while it's on screen, keeping any tables you've opened. Leaving it open doesn't keep you signed in: you're still signed out after the usual idle hours.
