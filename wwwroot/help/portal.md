## Signing in

Use your school email and the password the IT team gave you. The first time, you'll be asked to choose your own password.

Tick **Keep me signed in** only on your own computer or laptop. On a shared or staffroom computer leave it unticked - you're signed out when the browser closes, or after two hours without using the portal.

Forgotten your password? Ask the IT team to set you a new one.

## Reporting a problem

1. Press **Report a problem**.
2. If you're asked **who can help** (IT, Site, Cleaning...), tap the team. Not sure? Choose **Something else**.
3. Tap the **kind of problem**, then the **closest match** to what's wrong.
4. Say **where it is** (the room), **how urgent it is**, and anything else that would help under **Tell us more**.
5. Add a **photo** if it helps - a picture of an error message saves a lot of back and forth.
6. Press **Submit**.

If you can't see your problem in the list, choose **Something else** and type it in your own words.

> **Tip:** A good report says what happened, where, and what you were doing - for example *Projector in B12 shows "No signal" when the laptop is plugged in*.

## Is it urgent?

The portal is for things that can wait a little. If someone could be hurt, there's a leak, or a lesson can't go ahead right now, **phone or radio** as well, then report it here so there's a record.

## Checking on something you reported

**My tickets** lists everything you've reported, newest first, with its status. A **new** badge means the team have replied and you haven't read it yet.

Open a ticket to see the conversation and its status, such as *Open*, *On Hold* (waiting for something, perhaps a part or an answer from you) or *Closed*.

## Replying and adding photos

Open the ticket, type under **Add a message**, add any photos, and press **Send**. Reply when the team ask you something - the ticket may be waiting on you.

## When it's been fixed

The team close the ticket, often with a message saying what they did.

- If it isn't fixed after all, add a message within a few days and the ticket **reopens** so the team see it.
- After that, it can't take new messages - press **Report it again**, and the team will see it's linked to the old one.

## Notifications

**Notifications** (on the portal home page) can give you a ping and a pop-up on this computer when the team reply to you. Switch them on there; they only work while a portal page is open in your browser.

## Requesting a project

If you're a line manager or on SLT, you may be able to **request a project**: ask the IT team to find something to buy, gather quotes and put together a proposal.

1. Press **Request a project**.
2. Give it a **title**, say **when you need the proposal by**, and list **what you want** - as much detail as you have (how many, for whom, any must-haves).
3. Tick any **purchasing requirements**, such as a lease option.
4. Choose **how urgent it is**, honestly - it helps urgent work reach the right person first.
5. Press **Send to the IT team**.

**My projects** shows how it's going and who's working on it. You can add messages, and once the proposal is ready you can **download the proposal (PDF)**. Quotes and prices stay with the IT team until then.

## For the project lead

If you're the **project lead**, you see every project, with new ones under *Awaiting assignment*. Open one, confirm its **priority**, and choose the **technician** - the list shows how much each already has on. You can raise projects yourself, and the priority you choose stands.

## Changing your password

On the portal home page, choose **Change password**, enter your current password and a new one twice. Three random words together make a good password.
